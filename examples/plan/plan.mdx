# Refactor plan

We're cleaning up auth. Three phases.

| Phase | What | Effort | Risk |
| ----- | ---- | ------ | ---- |
| 1 | Extract auth middleware into a package | 1 day | low |
| 2 | Migrate session store to Postgres | 3 days | medium |
| 3 | Drop the Redis dependency | 1 hour | low |

## Why now

The current middleware leaks session tokens through query params. Legal flagged it.
Phases 1 and 2 are sequential — we can't drop Redis until sessions live in Postgres.

## Out of scope

- Rotating the signing key (separate ticket)
- Adding 2FA (Q3 roadmap)
